Workflow

Our goal is to propose reliable and automated methods to model solar masks from geodatasets. We aim at finding the best compromise for practitioners between precision of results and time needed for calculations. We explore different 2,5D and 3D modelling techniques for building and vegetation solar masks. We then use each as solar masks in building dynamic thermal simulations and compare simulation results' precision and time needed for calculations.

1. Explore geodatasets describing buildings' and/or trees' volume
2. Experiment 2,5D and 3D modelling methods to create building solar masks
3. Experiment 2,5D and 3D modelling methods to create vegetation solar masks
